Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

BilPay is currently a pre-launch information website. This page is a preliminary policy draft and must be reviewed and updated before any customer data is collected or financial service is launched.

1. Information on this website

This website is provided to explain the BilPay project and its planned services. It does not currently provide payment, transfer, account opening or banking functionality.

2. Information collection

This information website does not intentionally request payment credentials, PINs, passwords, OTPs, bank account details or identity documents. Do not submit such information through unofficial channels.

3. Future services

If BilPay introduces account-based or payment services, a revised privacy notice will explain what personal and transaction information is collected, why it is used, the lawful basis for processing, retention periods, and the rights available to users.

4. Service providers and partners

Any future sharing of information with regulated financial institutions, payment providers or technical service providers will be described in the applicable service-specific privacy notice and governed by relevant agreements and laws.

5. Security

BilPay will publish details of applicable security measures before launching services that process personal or financial information. Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone.

6. Contact

Official privacy contact details will be published before customer services launch.
